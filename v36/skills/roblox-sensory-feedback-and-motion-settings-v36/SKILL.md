# Roblox sensory feedback and motion settings

Use this skill for combat-free gameplay feedback, navigation, alerts, tutorials, progress, menus, and accessibility settings.

## Workflow
1. Identify every critical cue and the information it communicates.
2. Pair critical audio with a visual or text alternative; pair visual-only alerts with an optional audio cue when appropriate.
3. Avoid flashing patterns and unnecessarily intense or continuous motion.
4. Offer reduced-motion behavior for nonessential camera movement, transitions, and decorative effects.
5. Ensure reduced-motion settings persist predictably and apply consistently across screens.
6. Provide volume controls and avoid making essential progress depend on hearing a sound.
7. Test cues with sound muted, effects reduced, and on lower-performance devices.
8. Document what was tested and what remains unverified.

## Output
Produce a cue inventory, alternative-feedback matrix, motion-setting behavior, and device test plan.

## Boundaries
Do not use accessibility settings to profile players. Do not remove essential gameplay information when motion or audio is disabled.
