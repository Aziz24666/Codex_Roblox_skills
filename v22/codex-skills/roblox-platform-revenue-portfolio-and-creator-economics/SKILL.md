# Platform revenue portfolio and creator economics

## Mission
Map the earning paths that genuinely fit the experience, audience, production capacity, and current Roblox rules. Do not optimize revenue at the cost of player trust.

## Workflow
1. Inventory relevant paths: fixed passes/products, subscriptions with delivered recurring value, Creator Rewards, eligible immersive ads, avatar items, and paid access where appropriate.
2. Verify each path against current official documentation and the experience's Creator Dashboard. Distinguish general features from account- or experience-gated programs.
3. Record prerequisites, implementation/operations cost, reporting source, payout uncertainty, policy dependencies, and player impact.
4. Separate player-paid sales, Roblox-funded rewards, advertising, and creator-marketplace earnings in the accounting model.
5. Track gross sales, platform treatment, pending/held earnings, adjustments, and realized Robux separately using current dashboard evidence.
6. Rank opportunities by player value, fit, delivery reliability, fairness, sustainability, and workload.
7. Model scenarios only from supplied aggregate observations; show assumptions and uncertainty. Never infer revenue from code keywords or guessed conversion.
8. Exclude options that are unavailable, non-compliant, unsupported by the game, or operationally unsustainable.

## Advertising safeguards
Treat ads as optional and policy-gated. Verify age/content suitability, account/experience eligibility, placement rules, and current format restrictions. Never disguise ads as game UI, manipulate clicks/teleports, or collect personal data for targeting.

## Constraints
No gambling, paid random outcomes, paid chance modifiers, artificial traffic, AFK loops, false scarcity, or individual spending-capacity targeting. Keep core play worthwhile without purchases or ads.

## Output
Return the earning-path matrix, verified/unknown eligibility, source links/check dates, operating cost, player impact, accounting definitions, and evidence-based recommendation.
