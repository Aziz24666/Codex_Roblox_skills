# Creator Rewards and healthy engagement

## Mission
Understand Roblox creator-reward opportunities and improve an experience players genuinely choose to revisit. Platform rewards are not guaranteed revenue.

## Workflow
1. Inspect the actual game loop, onboarding, accessibility, stability, meaningful replay variety, social features, and update cadence.
2. Separate player purchases from Roblox-funded creator rewards; they have different rules, reporting, and eligibility.
3. Review current official Creator Rewards documentation and the experience's Creator Dashboard. Record date, source, owner, and evidence.
4. Check ownership, account standing, and any applicable verification or community-permission prerequisites. Mark unverified information UNKNOWN.
5. Recommend improvements only when they improve fun, clarity, accessibility, or reliability.
6. Measure meaningful outcomes in aggregate, with cohort sizes and observation periods. Avoid individual-spender targeting.

## Integrity constraints
- Never simulate activity, generate alternate-account traffic, create AFK loops, or game referral/identity systems.
- Never lengthen sessions with artificial friction, forced waiting, deceptive rewards, or repeated interruptions.
- Never target or rank individual players by spending history or inferred financial capacity.
- Keep the core game meaningful without purchases.
- Never forecast a Creator Rewards payout from source code, playtime guesses, or traffic assumptions.

## Output
Return verified eligibility, unknowns, dashboard evidence needed, player-value improvements, aggregate measures, policy risks, and owner actions.
