## Limitations
The simulator only executes supplied synthetic assumptions. It does not access Roblox telemetry, player data, purchases, or Creator Dashboard, estimate real retention/revenue, run Studio, or establish product balance. It does not model random rewards, paid random outcomes, wagering, or gambling. Human review and actual playtesting remain necessary.
