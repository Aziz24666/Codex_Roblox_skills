# Privacy-conscious feedback collection

## Before collection

Explain the purpose, participation choice, data categories, access, retention, and contact route in clear language. Provide a genuine skip/stop option. Obtain platform, legal, guardian, or organizational approvals where required for the intended audience and method.

## Data minimization

Prefer a controlled category selection and observed task outcome. Do not collect usernames, user IDs, email addresses, contact information, IP addresses, device fingerprints, precise location, credentials, chat transcripts, screenshots, or unrestricted free text in the V44 dataset. Do not join research submissions to persistent player profiles or unrelated analytics.

The CLI rejects fields outside the declared schema. This is a file-shape safeguard, not PII detection: a person could still place sensitive text inside an allowed label or opaque reference. Use only the predefined values and inspect the collection process itself.

## Access and retention

Keep any original research material in an approved system with least-privilege access. The repository stores only coarse codes and study-scoped opaque references. Keep any reference mapping separately under approved access controls; never put the mapping into the repository. Set a short retention period and verify deletion.

## Unexpected sensitive content

Do not copy a disclosure into the research file, and do not ask an automated system to assess it. Use the established human safety/moderation process. Retain only the minimum authorized case reference necessary for a controlled handoff.

## Honest claims

A declared “no identifiers” field does not prove that the collection method avoided identifiers. The study owner must verify the UI, survey provider, logs, exports, permissions, and deletion process.
