# Feedback study design

## Purpose and decision boundary

Begin with a specific decision: what part of the experience may change, what is unknown, and what new evidence would change the plan? Do not start by asking for general opinions about everything. A focused question produces more useful observations and reduces unnecessary collection.

State the build identifier, environment, study window, method, eligible audience, owner role, and intended decision. Keep the audience description coarse and avoid sensitive or identifying attributes.

## Methods

- Moderated playtest: observe a participant attempting a defined task. Record task stage, outcome, and structured friction categories.
- Structured survey: ask a small set of neutral questions after the relevant interaction. Make answers optional and avoid leading language.
- Support summary: code recurring issue categories from approved, access-controlled summaries. This is useful for issue discovery, not prevalence measurement.

Do not combine unrelated questions or change the study objective after seeing early results. Record major deviations and explain their effect on interpretation.

## Sampling and limits

Document recruitment source, eligibility, target response count, expected device coverage, language coverage, and known exclusions. Convenience samples and volunteer surveys are self-selected. A minimum count is a study planning gate, not a guarantee of statistical representativeness.

Set a small-cell threshold before collection. If a subgroup or category falls below it, suppress the count in exported summaries. Avoid repeated slicing that could reveal a participant indirectly.

## Neutral prompts

Ask about a recent, concrete interaction. Prefer “What part of the task was unclear?” over “Was the new tutorial much better?” Separate observed task failure from personal preference and feature requests.

## Safety route

Player-safety reports, threats, harassment, exploit disclosures, and sensitive personal disclosures are not ordinary research feedback. Do not copy their content into the research dataset. Route them through the established, authorized safety or incident workflow.
