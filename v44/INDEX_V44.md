# Roblox Codex Game Studio Kit — V44

V44.0.0 is the cumulative V1–V44 package with **280 native Codex skills**. Download only the current release.

## V44 — Player Feedback and Research Operations

Four new native Codex skills:

1. Feedback study design — define a focused research question, non-leading prompts, audience and sampling plan, study window, and decision owner.
2. Privacy-conscious collection — collect only structured, necessary feedback; avoid usernames, account IDs, contact details, raw free text, and persistent player tracking.
3. Qualitative coding and synthesis — apply a stable topic, sentiment, and impact taxonomy, record uncertainty, and separate observations from interpretation.
4. Feedback triage and action traceability — convert verified themes into scoped candidate actions with owners, evidence references, acceptance checks, and follow-up review.

## V44 offline CLI

v44/tools/v44.py provides doctor, validate-study, validate-data, audit, and summarize. It validates local research plans and coded, privacy-minimized feedback records. It creates descriptive aggregate summaries with small-cell suppression.

## Boundaries

V44 does not connect to Roblox, retrieve player data, collect or moderate feedback, authenticate source references, infer population-wide sentiment, estimate causal effects, create tasks in external systems, or change a live experience. A summary is not a representative survey result or a launch decision. Human review is required.

V1–V43 capabilities remain included.
