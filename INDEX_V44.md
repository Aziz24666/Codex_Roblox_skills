# Roblox Codex Game Studio Kit — V44

V44.0.0 is the cumulative V1–V44 package with **280 native Codex skills**. Download only the current release.

## V44 — Player Feedback and Research Operations

Four new skills:
- Focused, decision-oriented playtest and feedback study design.
- Privacy-conscious collection with voluntary participation, limited scope, short retention, and no unnecessary identifiers or unrestricted free text.
- Consistent qualitative coding, thematic synthesis, contradiction handling, and honest sample limitations.
- Human-owned triage, testable candidate actions, evidence traceability, and close-the-loop communication.

## Offline tooling

The V44 CLI validates study plans and coded feedback datasets, checks study windows and controlled vocabularies, flags leading prompt wording, suppresses small aggregate groups, and writes descriptive summaries. It does not collect feedback or inspect original source material.

## Safeguards

Do not store raw feedback, player identifiers, contact details, credentials, or sensitive disclosures in the project dataset. Use approved human safety/moderation workflows for sensitive reports. Descriptive feedback bands are not population estimates, causal evidence, or permission to ship a change.

All V1–V43 capabilities remain included.
