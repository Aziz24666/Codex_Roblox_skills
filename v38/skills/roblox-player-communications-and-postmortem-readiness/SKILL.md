# Player communications and postmortem readiness

## Player communication
1. Confirm a notice is actually required and identify the authorized communications owner.
2. Use confirmed facts only: affected feature/service, approximate time window, current status, workaround if verified, and next update point.
3. Do not speculate about root cause, promise a resolution time without evidence, expose internal security details, or blame individuals.
4. Do not include player identities, support excerpts, private account details, credentials, exploit reproduction details, or unverified compensation claims.
5. Have the notice reviewed by an authorized human before publication. Record a reference to the approved message; this skill does not send it.

## Closure
Do not mark an incident resolved until the service has been observed healthy for an appropriate window, customer impact is closed or explicitly handed off, verification evidence exists, and required player communication has been reviewed. Critical/high incidents require a postmortem reference and follow-up ownership.

## Blameless postmortem
Capture summary, impact window, detection, timeline, contributing factors, what worked, what delayed response, recovery/verification, and owned follow-up actions with due dates. Focus on system conditions and safeguards, not personal blame.

## Output
A fact-checked notice draft, closure checklist, postmortem outline, and follow-up register.

## Limits
This skill does not contact players, publish notices, query Roblox, verify service health, authenticate evidence, or assign blame.
