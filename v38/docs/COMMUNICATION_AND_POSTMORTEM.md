# Communication and postmortem readiness

## Player notice
Use confirmed facts only: the affected experience feature, a defensible time window, current status, a verified workaround if one exists, and when the next update is expected. Have an authorized communications owner review the notice. Avoid speculation, blame, unsupported timelines, internal security details, player information, and unverified compensation promises. Store an opaque reference to the approved notice; do not place its content or player reports in the incident record.

## Closure
Verify service health over a suitable observation window. Confirm customer impact has ended or is explicitly handed off. Require verification evidence. Critical/high incidents also need postmortem and follow-up references. A required notice must be reviewed before closure.

## Blameless postmortem
Record impact, timeline, detection, contributing system conditions, response effectiveness, recovery evidence, and owned follow-up actions. Use clear action owners and due dates. Focus on improving systems and safeguards, not blaming individuals.
