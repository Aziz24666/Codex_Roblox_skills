# V23.0.0 — Monetization Opportunity Discovery and Sustainable Growth

V23 grows the complete cumulative toolkit to 201 native Codex skills and adds a project-wide monetization discovery system.

Highlights:
- Search 48 deterministic monetization routes across the whole game project and return ranked evidence with exact source paths and line numbers.
- Discover beyond passes and developer products: fixed cosmetics, authored content, subscriptions, private servers, Creator Rewards, eligible immersive ads, avatar products, affiliate opportunities, and Creator Store assets/plugins.
- Design healthier player-return loops around onboarding, mastery, collections with fixed rewards, co-op, social identity, meaningful live operations, and accessibility.
- Improve store discovery with Roblox product-intelligence APIs, clear product details, dynamic price display, deliberate purchase actions, and server-authoritative grants.
- Track aggregate conversion, ARPDAU, ARPPU, revenue mix, subscriptions, retention, and support outcomes without profiling individual players.
- Preserve the mandatory no-gambling rule, deterministic product outcomes, fair free progression, and no-pressure shop rules.

V23 does not promise or estimate Robux. Creator Dashboard eligibility, live Studio behavior, marketplace APIs, and actual earnings must be verified separately.
