# Interpreting V39 simulation results

## Currency fields
- `source_earned`: units generated by modeled gameplay sources.
- `progression_rewards`: deterministic rewards granted when XP thresholds are reached.
- `sink_paid`: modeled sink demand actually paid from available balance.
- `sink_shortfall`: sink demand that could not be paid because the cohort lacked currency.
- `closing_balance`: remaining synthetic balance at the end of the horizon.

## Warnings
- A sink shortfall may indicate a progression gate or sink that is too expensive for the modeled activity profile. It is not automatically a bug.
- A balance above the healthy ceiling may indicate weak sinks, overly generous sources, or an unrealistic ceiling.
- A currency with sources but no sinks tends to accumulate in this simplified model.
- A progression gap between activity archetypes is a review signal, not a target to eliminate. Fairness may require accessible paths for casual players.

## Iteration
Change one parameter at a time, rerun, compare reports, and document the decision. Check exploitability, server authority, repeatable rewards, alternate progression paths, and whether paid purchases are required to access essential play.

## Never infer
Do not treat synthetic cohort results as observed player behavior, retention, conversion, revenue, or lifetime value. Do not individualize cohorts by spend or identity. This simulator excludes random paid outcomes and gambling mechanics by design.
