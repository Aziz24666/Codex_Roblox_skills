# V23 Overlay Index

## Five specialist skills
- roblox-monetization-opportunity-discovery — find and rank the widest evidence-supported revenue portfolio.
- roblox-value-led-engagement-design — improve repeat play through meaningful, player-chosen game value.
- roblox-store-product-discovery-and-conversion — product intelligence, shop clarity, and purchase funnel quality.
- roblox-revenue-analytics-and-experimentation — aggregate metrics, safe tests, and revenue attribution.
- roblox-monetization-growth-compliance — scale monetization without gambling, coercion, policy violations, or unreliable fulfillment.

## CLI
- tools/v23.py discover-opportunities — search project code and design files against the full monetization opportunity catalogue. It excludes toolkit/session/build metadata, skips symlinks, and only writes reports inside the selected project root.
- Optional scan bounds: `--max-files` and `--max-file-bytes`.
- tools/v23.py evaluate-routing — evaluate V23 skill-routing regressions.
- tools/v23.py verify — validate catalogue, routing, and cumulative package.

## Interpretation
Fit scores show the density of project evidence and are not forecasts, conversion estimates, eligibility decisions, or guaranteed Robux. Every opportunity requires design review and player-value validation.
