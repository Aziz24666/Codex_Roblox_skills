# Value-led engagement design

## Mission
Increase genuine enjoyment, retention, and reasons to return. The store should benefit from a good game; the game must never become a funnel whose primary purpose is to pressure spending.

## Workflow
1. Map the first-session experience, main gameplay loop, skill progression, replay variety, collections, co-op/social systems, live-ops cadence, and accessibility.
2. Identify friction, bugs, empty goals, weak onboarding, content gaps, and performance problems before adding monetization.
3. Design repeatable play: mastery goals, authored challenges with fixed rewards, rotating content, optional collections, community events, co-op objectives, and player-created identity.
4. Connect optional purchases only to value already present: cosmetics, expression, extra presets, clearly defined authored content, or recurring benefits that can be delivered.
5. Keep the free path enjoyable and viable. Provide earned paths to core gameplay items and never make purchases mandatory to progress.
6. Measure aggregate D1/D7/D30 retention, completion, returning cohorts, session quality, and opt-in social participation.
7. Treat Creator Rewards as a platform program, not a controllable per-player payout. Verify eligibility and never engineer sessions to meet a time threshold.
8. Review changes with players and stop features that create frustration, excessive interruptions, or pressure.

## Forbidden patterns
No AFK farms, artificial session extensions, forced waiting, attendance penalties, lost streaks that shame players, countdown pressure, repeated purchase popups, or individual spender profiling. Do not force advertising or gate essential gameplay behind ads.

## Output
Return a healthy engagement loop map, player-value hypothesis, free/premium boundaries, content cadence, aggregate metrics, fairness risks, and a test plan.

## Shared contracts
Preserve V18 no-gambling, V19 evidence, V20 server-authority, V21 fair monetization, and V22 platform-monetization rules.

## V23 value-led monetization and opportunity coverage contract

- Search the widest relevant monetization catalogue and rank by game evidence, genuine player value, cost, fairness, and verified demand; never forecast Robux from source code.
- Keep player purchases, Creator Rewards, ads, avatar/affiliate income, and Creator Store income separate with separate eligibility and accounting.
- Improve retention through fun, stable, accessible, social, replayable content; never create artificial playtime, AFK loops, forced attendance, coercive prompts, or spending-based player profiling.
- Preserve V18 no-gambling: no paid random outcomes, mystery purchases, paid chance modifiers, spins, wagers, paid rerolls, or indirect Robux-funded random reward paths.
- Keep the core game enjoyable without spending; paid outcomes must be deterministic, optional, fixed, and clearly disclosed.
- Use aggregate analytics and current platform documentation; mark live Studio, Creator Dashboard, marketplace, eligibility, receipt, and subscription checks NOT_RUN or BLOCKED until evidenced.
