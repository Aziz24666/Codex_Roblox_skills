# Monetization growth and compliance

## Mission
Expand sustainable creator earnings while protecting players, respecting player choice, and following current Roblox platform requirements.

## Workflow
1. Check current Creator Hub documentation and Creator Dashboard for each monetization route and the experience's eligibility.
2. Inventory product presentation, pricing, receipts, grants, subscriptions, ads, private servers, and Creator Store listings.
3. Apply V18 no-gambling and fair-monetization rules as hard gates; odds disclosures never relax this toolkit's stricter policy.
4. Reject fake discounts, false scarcity, countdown pressure, hidden prices, coercive prompts, forced ads, artificial waiting, AFK farming, and session manipulation.
5. Preserve an enjoyable free game. Purchases are optional, precise, and user-initiated.
6. Ensure server-authoritative catalog, receipts, entitlements, inventory changes, subscriptions, and persistence. Make grants idempotent and recoverable.
7. Check eligibility without bypassing age, identity, location, account-standing, or experience requirements. Never use bots, alternate accounts, or traffic manipulation.
8. Keep earnings categories separate and use aggregate analytics with retention guardrails.

## Release gates
- Every paid offer has a known fixed outcome and truthful contents.
- Recurring products disclose cadence, benefits, and cancellation/expiry behavior.
- Product price display agrees with current platform price and purchase prompt.
- Receipts, grants, persistence, rejoin reconciliation, and rollback are tested.
- Platform eligibility and actual Creator Dashboard settings are evidenced.
- No live check is marked PASS without actual Studio/dashboard evidence.

## Output
Produce a compliance matrix, portfolio gaps, policy risks, eligibility unknowns, server-integrity checks, evidence requirements, and GO/NO-GO result.

## Shared contracts
Preserve V18 no-gambling, V19 evidence, V20 server-authority, V21 fair monetization, and V22 platform-monetization rules.

## V23 value-led monetization and opportunity coverage contract

- Search the widest relevant monetization catalogue and rank by game evidence, genuine player value, cost, fairness, and verified demand; never forecast Robux from source code.
- Keep player purchases, Creator Rewards, ads, avatar/affiliate income, and Creator Store income separate with separate eligibility and accounting.
- Improve retention through fun, stable, accessible, social, replayable content; never create artificial playtime, AFK loops, forced attendance, coercive prompts, or spending-based player profiling.
- Preserve V18 no-gambling: no paid random outcomes, mystery purchases, paid chance modifiers, spins, wagers, paid rerolls, or indirect Robux-funded random reward paths.
- Keep the core game enjoyable without spending; paid outcomes must be deterministic, optional, fixed, and clearly disclosed.
- Use aggregate analytics and current platform documentation; mark live Studio, Creator Dashboard, marketplace, eligibility, receipt, and subscription checks NOT_RUN or BLOCKED until evidenced.
