# Revenue analytics and experimentation

## Mission
Use honest aggregate evidence to understand what players value and which offers work. Do not claim sales, forecast Robux, or attribute causality without data.

## Metrics
- Daily/weekly active users and new, returning, and reactivated cohorts.
- D1, D7, and D30 retention; onboarding completion; meaningful gameplay completion.
- Store visit, product detail view, purchase prompt, completed purchase, successful grant, and support/error rates.
- Payer conversion, ARPDAU, ARPPU, gross Robux, net credited Robux, and product-type breakdown where the dashboard provides them.
- Subscription starts, renewals, cancellations, expiry, benefit delivery, and churn.
- Ad impressions/views/teleports and revenue only where ads are eligible and enabled.

## Workflow
1. Define the question, aggregate cohort, sample size, time window, baseline, guardrails, and owner before changing the experience.
2. Separate player purchases from Creator Rewards, advertising, affiliate/marketplace income, and Creator Store sales.
3. Use Creator Dashboard analytics as the source of platform earnings and eligibility; do not infer payout from scripts or estimated traffic.
4. Test shop layout, product clarity, previews, onboarding, and content discovery with aggregate measurements.
5. Use Roblox Managed Pricing only for passes and developer products when eligible and dynamically scripted; it does not apply to subscriptions.
6. Report confidence and limitations. Small samples are exploratory, correlation is not causation, and short-term revenue lift can be outweighed by lower retention or trust.
7. Never collect unnecessary personal data, rank individual players by spend, or target financially vulnerable players.

## Output
Return metric definitions, baseline, cohort/sample notes, observed results, retention/fairness guardrails, experiments, and next actions. Leave unknown metrics UNKNOWN and future tests NOT_RUN.
