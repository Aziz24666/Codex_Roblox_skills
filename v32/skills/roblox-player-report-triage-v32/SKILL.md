# Player report triage

## Workflow

1. Preserve Roblox's built-in Report Abuse flow and authorized Creator Dashboard safety tools.
2. Publish clear experience rules, report categories, reviewer roles, and a private appeal route.
3. Treat every report as a lead to investigate, not proof of a violation.
4. Triage using documented severity and response-time policy, not popularity, spending, identity, or retaliation.
5. Keep original identities and evidence inside authorized Roblox moderation systems.
6. Use V32 case format only for opaque references, fixed codes, severity, channel, timestamps, and review metadata.
7. Escalate urgent or high-impact matters to authorized human reviewers under the published policy.
8. Review aggregate safety signals by feature/channel while avoiding player-level dossiers.

## Quality rules

- Do not copy player IDs, usernames, chat, voice, screenshots, personal details, or free-text allegations into Codex files.
- Do not replace the official platform report path with an obstructive custom-only report flow.
- Do not sanction a player solely based on report volume or an automated heuristic.
- Keep records private, access-controlled, and subject to a documented retention schedule.

## Safety

Every case needs a fair review and a meaningful correction/appeal path. Keep all V1-V31 safeguards.
