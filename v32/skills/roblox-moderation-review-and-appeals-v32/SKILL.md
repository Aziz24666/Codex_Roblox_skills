# Moderation review and appeals

## Workflow

1. Read the published rule and identify the exact policy category being reviewed.
2. Examine only original evidence available through authorized Roblox moderation systems.
3. Separate confirmed observations from allegations, assumptions, and missing context.
4. Record the case using V32 predefined summary and decision codes; never paste raw evidence into the case file.
5. Use a proportionate, policy-based outcome and document who reviewed it by role.
6. Require an independent second reviewer for severities selected in the project's policy.
7. Explain the action and the appeal route in clear, respectful language.
8. Reconsider appeals with fresh review; record whether the decision was upheld, modified, or overturned.
9. Check reviewer access and retention regularly.

## Quality rules

- Never automate sanctions from report counts, static source findings, or unverified signals.
- Never publicly expose accusations, identities, evidence, or moderation history.
- Never retaliate against a reporter or moderator.
- Use Roblox-supported moderation controls only through authorized human-operated workflows and current API guidance.

## Safety

V32 validates documentation and metadata; it does not decide violations, ban players, or execute moderation actions. Preserve all V1-V31 safeguards.
