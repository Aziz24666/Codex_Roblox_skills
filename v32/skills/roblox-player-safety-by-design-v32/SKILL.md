# Player safety by design

## Workflow

1. List every user-generated-content surface and who can create, edit, publish, view, and report it.
2. Consider predefined choices, private drafts, pre-moderation, trusted-user gates, and limited-reach sharing before public publication.
3. Make shared content reversible or ephemeral when permanent publication is unnecessary.
4. Keep permissions, ownership, placement, dimensions, and rate limits server-authoritative.
5. Retain Roblox report/block tools and make experience rules accessible to every player.
6. Review aggregate safety signals after launch and revisit any feature with a sustained increase in abuse reports.
7. Complete the content ratings questionnaire accurately and update it when the experience changes.
8. Re-run the V32 checklist whenever a new social or UGC feature ships.

## Design safeguards

- Avoid features that solicit personal information or pressure players to move conversations off-platform.
- Avoid public accusation boards, retaliation, or shame-based enforcement.
- Keep social participation optional and understandable.
- Avoid designs that create unsafe private settings or enable misuse of avatars and shared assets.

## Quality gate

Treat safety review as part of feature acceptance and production release readiness. Do not claim a feature is safe merely because a text scan passes.
