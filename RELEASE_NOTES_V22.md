# V22.0.0 — Platform monetization readiness and creator economics

V22 extends V1–V21 with five specialists, a static Luau pricing-readiness auditor, subscription-contract schema and validator, five routing regressions, cumulative skill audits, and reproducible release packaging.

## What's new
- Creator Rewards eligibility review and healthy engagement improvements.
- Managed Pricing, regional-price readiness, dynamic display, and transfer safeguards.
- Subscription lifecycle, recurring benefit delivery, server-confirmed entitlements, and idempotent period grants.
- Platform revenue portfolio planning with distinct player-purchase, creator-reward, advertising, and marketplace accounting.
- Monetization release gate for policy, evidence, support, and rollback.
- CLI for price-literal/API scanning and subscription-contract validation.

## Constraints
V18's no-gambling and fair-monetization rules remain mandatory. V22 adds no gambling, paid random outcomes, paid chance modifiers, artificial activity, manipulative session-length optimization, or individual-spender targeting.

## Evidence boundaries
The scanner cannot verify dashboard eligibility, Managed Pricing configuration, price-test qualification, live subscriptions, or actual earnings. Complete live checks in an authorized Roblox environment and record them under V19 evidence rules.
