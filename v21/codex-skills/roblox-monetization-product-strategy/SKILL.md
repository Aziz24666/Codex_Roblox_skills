# Roblox monetization product strategy

## When to use
Use after project discovery to choose which direct-purchase offers genuinely belong in the experience.

## Strategy
1. Tie every offer to a real player need or optional expression supported by the game: identity, creativity, social expression, convenience, ongoing content, or private play.
2. Prefer cosmetics and customization before power advantages. Preserve fair competition and the normal progression route.
3. Choose a one-time pass only for a stable, clearly described entitlement. Choose a repeatable developer product only when it grants a precise item, quantity, or known fixed benefit.
4. Use subscriptions only when the team can continuously deliver the exact ongoing benefits, support entitlement changes, and clearly explain renewal and cancellation.
5. Use timed benefits only where the exact effect and duration are fixed, independently testable, and visible before purchase. Never tie the effect to odds or uncertain rewards.
6. Use private-server products only when private play has real value and the experience can support it.
7. Avoid adding an offer when its main purpose is to repair an intentionally frustrating loop or make essential progression unnecessarily slow.

## Product opportunity review
For each idea record: audience need, supporting mechanic, exact grant, purchase class, price owner/source, duration, prerequisites, earnable alternative, server implementation, refund/support implications, and analytics. Reject ideas with no evidence of player value.

## Revenue reasoning
Rank opportunities by game-fit, likely reach based on measured exposure, player value, delivery reliability, and operational cost. Do not infer purchase propensity from an individual player's age, location, device, or vulnerability. Do not project revenue from code structure alone.

## Required references
Refresh official Roblox Creator Hub guidance for the exact purchase class before implementation. Consider platform-managed price optimization and regional pricing where available; never assume they are active or enabled without verifying the game's Creator Dashboard configuration.

## Output contract
Provide an evidence-backed offer portfolio, a low-risk rollout order, product requirements, analytics requirements, and clear reject/hold decisions.

## V18 shared workflow and handoff contract
Preserve shared workflow contracts and the mandatory no-gambling economy rule.

## V19 verification and evaluation contract
Require risk-based tests and honest evidence states.

## V20 production reliability contract
Use server-owned product definitions, verified receipts, idempotent fixed grants, and safe rollouts.

## V21 sustainable monetization contract
Offers must provide clear value and remain optional. Revenue optimization must not depend on pressure, deception, or gambling-like mechanics.
